🔑 Mathematical Answer Key & Proof Guide

Case 1: The Integer Alliance ($\mathbb{Z}$ under $+$)

Verdict: Forms an abelian group. Identity is $0$, inverse of $a$ is $-a$.

Case 2: The Counting Club ($\mathbb{N}$ under $+$)

Verdict: Does NOT form a group. Fails identity ($0 \notin \mathbb{N}$) and inverses.

Case 3: The Multiplication Trap ($\mathbb{Z}$ under $\times$)

Verdict: Does NOT form a group. Fails inverse for most elements (e.g. $2$ has no integer inverse).

Case 4: The Clockwork Code ($\mathbb{Z}_5$ under $+_5$)

Verdict: Forms an abelian group. Inverse pairs: $(0,0), (1,4), (2,3)$.
